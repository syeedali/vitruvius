Open-source engineering research toolkit

Engineering research that shows its work.

Vitruvius turns engineering questions into evidence-backed briefs — sources opened, artifacts anchored, claims checked, and uncertainty recorded.

A summary is not a decision

A fluent answer can still be impossible to audit. Vitruvius makes the evidence trail part of the output.

01

Read the source

A claim is capped by what was actually read. A snippet cannot support a verified value; a page locator can.

02

Ground the claim

Repository findings point at hash-pinned bytes through a real path:line anchor, not a filename that merely exists.

03

Verify independently

A blind verifier checks the conclusion against the gathered evidence. Failed tools and unconfirmed findings fail closed.

The research loop

One method. Every run.

The final gate re-derives the original question. A run is not done because the prose sounds finished.

  1. I

    Plan

    Question, artifacts, decisions, evidence.

  2. II

    Scale

    Direct search or parallel researchers.

  3. III

    Gather

    Open sources and record read depth.

  4. IV

    Draft

    Decision-first brief with citations.

  5. V

    Cite

    Sweep claims back to exact sources.

  6. VI

    Review

    Blind verification and adversarial review.

  7. VII

    Deliver

    Goal-check, brief, provenance, anchors.
