The command catalog

Twenty-seven commands. One method.

Start with the job you need done. Every research command ends at the same evidence trail and delivery gate.

Entry points

Start here

Let Vitruvius choose the discipline or workflow, or run the shared method directly.

Dispatch

Shared method

Discover, read, synthesize, verify, review, and deliver.

Named research jobs

Workflow skills

Use a specialized skill when you know the job; use engineering-research when you do not.

/gap-analysis

Find gaps

Identify a literature gap through scholarly triangulation.

/evidence-ranking

Rank evidence

Score evidence by quality and applicability.

/verifier

Verify

Check a claim or calculation against authoritative sources.

/design-alternatives

Alternatives

Compare three or more approaches with scored trade-offs.

/fmea-brainstorm

FMEA

Identify failure modes with severity, occurrence, detection, and RPN.

/hypothesis-generation

Hypotheses

Freeze rival hypotheses with evidence boundaries and tests.

/peer-review

Peer review

Produce severity-graded findings and a revision plan.

/compare

Compare

Build a source-grounded comparison matrix.

/review

Review

Run an adversarial internal critique of an artifact.

/audit

Audit

Compare paper, specification, or design claims against implementation.

/summarize

Summarize

Extract a faithful digest with numbers, units, and caveats.

/research-to-post

Research to post

Draft two or three hook posts and a copy-first HTML card.

/eli5

Explain simply

Explain an engineering concept in plain language.

/simplified-technical-english

Controlled language

Write and check technical text against ASD-STE100.

/artifact-reading

Read an artifact

Read PDFs, drawings, specifications, and code with anchored extraction.

/scholarly-research

Literature

Synthesize consensus, disagreements, trajectories, and source status.

/standards-lookup

Standards

Retrieve the governing provision with edition and section.

/habit

Habit

Capture explicit research preferences for human approval.
