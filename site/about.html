About

Research you can audit.

A research answer is useful only when another person can see what was read, what was inferred, what was blocked, and which evidence supports the decision.

Mission

Make engineering research auditable

Vitruvius is an open-source toolkit for coding agents. It runs a disciplined research workflow over engineering questions and artifacts, then leaves a trail another person can check.

01

Commands

Twenty-seven entry points for research, verification, standards, literature, comparison, review, audit, and design analysis.

02

Roles

Researcher, writer, verifier, reviewer, arbiter, goal-checker, and habit contracts keep the stages independent.

03

Contracts

Deterministic validators check read depth, provenance, artifact anchors, negative coverage, and final delivery.

Principles

Five rules that change the output

Read the source

Record whether the agent read metadata, a snippet, an abstract, a section, a page, a table, or the full document. A snippet cannot support a verified claim.

Ground the claim

A repository finding points at hash-pinned bytes. A verified absence claim must name the symbol it denies, and the anchored line must not declare it.

Show uncertainty

verified, partial, blocked, unverified, and inferred are different states, not decorations.

Verify independently

A blind verifier checks the conclusion against the evidence. The author does not grade their own work.

Land on a decision

Every finding names what to change, what to measure, what to defer, or why it is background. A paper list is not a synthesis.

Boundaries

Useful because it knows what it is not

The system is deliberately narrow. Its job is research discipline, not replacing engineering judgment.
It isIt is not
An evidence trail another person can inspectA guarantee that the model is always right
A research-only workflow for coding agentsFinal engineering sign-off or a compliance certificate
A way to make missing evidence visibleA substitute for reading the governing standard
A set of skills, roles, and deterministic validatorsA CAD, BIM, simulation, or general calculation engine
A project-local, review-gated preference loopSilent model training or hidden cross-project memory
Measured, not certified

01

Per-case variance is material

The checked-in benchmark is a measurement of one run, not a property guaranteed for every model or host.

02

Narrow anchors are not full comprehension

The contract can catch a missing anchor, an unsupported token, or an absence claim whose line declares the thing. It cannot read every sentence as a verifier can.

The name

One discipline, many sources

Vitruvius is named after Marcus Vitruvius Pollio, whose De Architectura treated architecture, engineering, machines, and materials as connected fields. The name reflects the project's premise: good decisions come from joining evidence across sources without losing the thread back to each one.
